Global Disparities in Vaccination Access

The Childhood Vaccination Gap in Central Africa

DTP3 tracks how many infants receive three doses of a vaccine that protects against diphtheria, tetanus and whooping cough.

Explore the Global Vaccination Map

Where Coverage Differs

Central Africa and the World

70% vs. 85%

Estimated DTP3 coverage in Central Africa and worldwide, respectively, in 2025.

Within Central Africa

46% to 89%

Estimated DTP3 coverage ranged from 46% in the Central African Republic to 89% in Burundi in 2025.

What Can Interrupt Vaccination?

Distance · Nkambe, Cameroon

3.2 times the odds

In a 2020 study of 385 children, those living less than 5 km from a vaccination site had 3.2 times the adjusted odds of completing vaccination compared with those living farther away.

Conflict · DR Congo

60%

In 2024, an estimated 60% of health facilities in conflict-affected areas were partly or fully out of operation. UNICEF reported disruptions to routine vaccination.

Reaching Children Who Missed Out

Catch-Up Vaccination · DR Congo

123,747

Children classified as zero-dose received vaccinations through UNICEF-supported work in four provinces in 2025.

SDG 3.b calls for access to affordable essential vaccines. DTP3 is one measure used to track progress under indicator 3.b.1. Immunization Agenda 2030 sets a global DTP3 coverage target of 90%.
